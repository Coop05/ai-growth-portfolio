# Portfolio presentation preferences

- Do not use em dashes in public copy, page titles, captions or documentation. Rewrite with a full stop, comma or separate sentence.
- Do not use emoji or Unicode arrow characters as decorative link or button suffixes.
- Prefer plain text links. Where an icon conveys a necessary control or direction, use a restrained inline SVG with an accessible label on the control.
- Preserve the soft red accent, mobile layout and reduced-motion support.


- Use one font family, Manrope, for all page text and controls. Different weights are fine. Do not mix serif, italic display faces or monospace labels into the page.
- Keep the sports theme integrated throughout a continuous 3D world, with playable tennis and racing interactions. Two isolated decorative objects do not meet the brief.
- Preserve all six original voxel fan characters: Federer, Nadal, Djokovic, Verstappen, Vettel and Schumacher.
