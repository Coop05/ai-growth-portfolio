# Forte Growth website

**[Visit the live site](https://www.fortegrowth.co/)** · [Back to portfolio](../README.md)

## The brief

Explain a broad GTM service clearly enough that a founder can recognise their situation, understand what the team does, inspect relevant evidence and choose a next step.

## My contribution

I built the Forte Growth website using AI-assisted development. The project is part of my work connecting commercial positioning with implementation and a usable customer journey.

## What to inspect

- **Customer situations:** the opening sections describe recognisable business problems.
- **Service scope:** outbound, content, founder brand, sales processes, automation and reporting are presented as connected work.
- **Delivery approach:** the site explains the sequence of work and ongoing review.
- **Product evidence:** the portal presentation makes the delivery experience more tangible.
- **Next step:** the diagnostic gives a visitor a way to investigate their own situation.

## Why it belongs in a growth portfolio

The site connects messaging, information structure, evidence and conversion paths in a shipped asset. It gives an interviewer something specific to explore: the intended audience, the choices made and how the experience could be tested and improved.
